About Cineplus+

Empowering local theater management with seamless online ticketing and cinema experiences.

Our Purpose & Mission

Cineplus+ was engineered as a comprehensive local theater management and web ticketing system designed to bridge modern online convenience with premium cinema projection. Our platform provides moviegoers with real-time seat reservation, streamlined concession bundling, and dynamic privilege discount integration (Student, Senior Citizen, and PWD) to ensure a hassle-free movie-going journey from home to the big screen.

Cinema House Policies

🎟️ Ticket Validity

E-tickets are non-transferable and valid only for the chosen movie slot and seat selection.

🍿 Concessions Policy

Only food and drinks purchased from Cineplus+ snack bars or combos are permitted inside cinema halls.

🎓 Privilege Discounts

Student (10%) and Senior/PWD (20%) discount holders must present valid government IDs upon entry verification.

🔇 Cinema Etiquette

Please set mobile phones to silent mode during film screenings. Unauthorized recording is strictly prohibited.
