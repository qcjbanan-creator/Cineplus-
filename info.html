CINEPLUS+

An integrated local cinema management and web ticketing engine engineered for seamless movie selection, concession ordering, automated discounts, and branch detection.

🍿 Purpose of the Platform

Cineplus+ bridges high-performance core C++ console logic with a modern, streaming-grade web front-end. Designed to streamline local theater reservations, Cineplus+ eliminates long queues by providing an all-in-one digital solution for reserving seats, selecting screening slots, and customizing concession orders.

🎟️

Automated Ticketing Engine

Select movie titles, preferred screening schedules (12:00 PM Matinee, 4:30 PM Prime, 8:00 PM Evening), and dedicated auditorium seating (Seats #01 through #18) with real-time seat tracking.

🎬

Curated Movie Catalog & Transparent Pricing

Browse blockbuster titles, detailed synopses, and fixed ticket rates (ranging from PHP 220.00 to PHP 250.00). Complete itemized breakdown gives full transparency before checkout.

🍿

Cinema Concessions & Food Bundles

Add individual theater snacks (Regular Popcorn, Bottled Soda, Hotdog Sandwiches) or cost-effective family snack packages (Solo Combo, Pair Combo, Family Bundle) directly to your booking cart.

🏷️

Automated Discount Module

Instant eligibility discounts applied at checkout:

  • Student Rate: 10% discount on total booking
  • Senior Citizen / PWD Rate: 20% mandatory discount
  • Regular Rate: Standard billing without surcharge
📍

Geolocation & Branch Detection

Integrated browser API tags your exact geographic coordinates (Latitude & Longitude) to automatically match and route your reservation to the nearest physical Cineplus+ theater branch.

💻

C++ Backend Foundation

Backed by underlying Object-Oriented C++ logic utilizing arrays, functions, structures, and memory-efficient algorithms to process ticket inventory, order calculations, and data persistence.
